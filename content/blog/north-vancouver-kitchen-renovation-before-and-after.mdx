Not every kitchen needs to be torn out — but this one had earned it. Built in the 1990s, this North Vancouver kitchen still worked, technically. It just no longer matched how the homeowners actually lived: dark cabinetry, a closed-in ceiling, and a layout that felt pieced together over the years rather than planned.

This is the story of how BRIO rebuilt it — raised ceiling, reworked layout, custom cabinetry, integrated appliances — into a brighter, calmer, more functional space. Below you'll find the before-and-after, the plan, and a look at the work that happens behind the finishes.

## The Homeowners' Challenge

The kitchen had the familiar feel of a room that had served the home for decades but stopped keeping up. Warm wood cabinetry, older tile floors, limited visual openness, and a layout that made the space feel more enclosed than connected.

The homeowners wanted a kitchen that was easier to use day to day — more practical storage, a cleaner cooking zone, brighter finishes, and a layout that felt intentional. Good natural light was there to build on; the room just wasn't taking advantage of it.

## The Plan: What We Set Out to Change

The goal wasn't a surface refresh — it was to change how the kitchen worked. We built the plan around a handful of clear priorities:

<CalloutCard tone="key" title="Project goals">

- Improve everyday functionality and flow
- A cleaner, brighter look
- More practical storage
- Modernized finishes and integrated appliances
- A kitchen that feels connected to the rest of the home

</CalloutCard>

To get there, the renovation touched the room itself, not just the finishes: the ceiling was raised to open the space up, the window size and level were adjusted, water lines were moved, the refrigerator was relocated for a built-in installation, and custom cabinetry was designed around how the homeowners actually cook and store.

## The Final Reveal

The finished kitchen introduces a softer, brighter palette with new cabinetry, slab surfaces, and a cleaner working layout. The relocated built-in refrigerator gives the room a more integrated feel, and the raised ceiling and reworked window make the whole space feel more connected to the home.
